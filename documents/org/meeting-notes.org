* The Thursday pager problem

  Six of the last eight out-of-hours pages have landed on a Thursday night,
  between 22:00 and 01:00. Nobody had noticed until Ines plotted them.

** Cause
   The weekly reconciliation job starts at 22:00 Thursday. It is not itself
   failing; it saturates the same connection pool the ingest path uses, and
   ingest is what pages.

** Options considered
   | Option                          | Effort | Risk   | Fixes it? |
   |---------------------------------+--------+--------+-----------|
   | Move the job to Sunday          | Low    | Low    | Moves it  |
   | Separate connection pools       | Medium | Low    | Yes       |
   | Rate-limit the reconciliation   | Low    | Medium | Mostly    |
   | Raise the pool size             | Low    | High   | No        |

** Decision
   Separate pools. Moving the job to Sunday was tempting and was rejected
   because it relocates the pain onto whoever is on call at the weekend, which
   is worse, not better.
